# ForgeOS Company Foundation

## Purpose
This directory contains the foundational specifications and documents for the ForgeOS organization. It establishes our vision, mission, philosophies, and long-term objectives.

## Scope
The scope of these documents encompasses the overarching strategy, culture, and high-level product direction of ForgeOS as an AI software engineering organization.

## Influence on Product Decisions
Every product decision must align with our [Product Philosophy](product-philosophy.md) and [Target Users](target-users.md). These documents serve as the litmus test for prioritizing features and designing user experiences.

## Influence on Architecture
Architectural choices are heavily guided by our [Engineering Philosophy](engineering-philosophy.md) and [Technology Philosophy](technology-philosophy.md), ensuring that we build systems that are modular, secure, and maintainable.

## Authoritative Documents
The documents within this directory (`/docs/01-company/`) are authoritative for strategic and foundational company matters. In cases of conflict, these specifications override individual component documentation.

## Approval Process
Changes to any document in this directory require review and sign-off from the Principal Engineer/CTO or equivalent executive human authority.

## Relationship with Governance
While `/docs/00-governance/` dictates *how* we build software (rules, processes, and standards), this directory (`/docs/01-company/`) defines *why* we build it and *what* we aim to achieve. The governance rules are the practical application of our company philosophies.
