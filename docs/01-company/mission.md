# Mission

Our mission is to democratize high-quality software engineering by making it accessible, reliable, and affordable for everyone.

We aim to empower:
- Students
- Individual developers
- Freelancers
- Startups
- Small businesses
- Engineering teams
- Enterprise organizations

### AI Amplifies Engineers
We believe that AI should amplify human engineers, not replace them. ForgeOS acts as a force multiplier, allowing a single developer to direct an entire organization of AI specialists.

### Human Control
No matter how advanced the AI becomes, humans retain control over critical, high-risk decisions. ForgeOS ensures that final approvals for deployments, massive refactoring, and security changes remain in the hands of the user.
